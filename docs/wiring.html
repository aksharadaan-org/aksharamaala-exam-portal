Future merge rehearsal

The mobile shell owns route decoding and the exam feature entry. The service adapter keeps networking outside screens. An integration harness mounts the entry in a second blank shell, injects a fake session and exercises start, logout, resume and exit. Later, the platform owner implements server-side identity mapping and contract adapters, validates staging with internally managed accounts and enables the host tile behind a feature flag. Roll back the flag independently of stored attempts. Synthetic IDs are disposable and must never identify real students.
